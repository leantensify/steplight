Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Steplight is made by Leantensify / BC Egemen Toprak (company ID 22240322), established in the Czech Republic. Questions about privacy go to support@leantensify.com.

The short version

  • Steplight has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and nothing in it tracks you.
  • What you write in Steplight is stored on your iPhone. We cannot read it.
  • Steplight makes no network calls of its own, with one exception. Purchases go through Apple.
  • The exception is the optional cloud coach. It is off until you turn it on, and while it is on, the task you are working on is sent to our server and to Anthropic to write each reply.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your tasks and their steps, routines, captured thoughts, the 48-hour list, rewards, wins and daily energy check-ins.
  • Focus sounds are made on the phone. Nothing is downloaded or streamed.
  • Your profile and settings: how ADHD shows up for you, the things you asked us to go gently with, the tone you chose, and your companion's name. These are self-described and stay on the phone. The optional cloud coach is sent only the tone, whether you asked for gentler wording, the companion's name, and a short list of what helps you start, which the phone works out from the rest.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings › Erase everything, or by deleting the app.

Backups

Steplight does not run a server that holds your tasks. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Steplight's data is part of that backup like any other app's, under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, small steps can be written by Apple's on-device model. That happens on the phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

The cloud coach (optional)

The cloud coach is part of Steplight Plus and stays off until you agree to it on the screen that asks. While it is on, we send:

  • the name of a task when its steps are written, together with what you said "done" looks like if you filled that in,
  • the name of the task and the step you are on when you start it with your companion, and what you tap or type during that session,
  • the tone you chose, whether you asked for gentler wording, and your companion's name,
  • a short list of what helps you start, chosen from five fixed phrases (starting with a movement, having the thing in front of you, extra-small steps, short named lengths of time, steady wording),
  • the app version, and Apple's signed proof that you have Plus.

We do not send how ADHD shows up for you, what trips you up, what you asked us to go gently with, your other tasks, your routines or anything that names you. The list of what helps you start is worked out on your phone from those answers, so it reflects them in a general way; the answers themselves are never sent.

If you use Empty your head with the cloud coach on, the text you type there is sent the same way so it can be split and sorted.

The request goes to our server, which runs on Cloudflare, and from there to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the reply. Our server does not store or log what you wrote. It keeps a counter, tied to your purchase and not to your name, to limit how many requests one membership can make; counters expire within about a day. Anthropic processes the text to produce the reply and may retain it for a limited period under its own terms. Cloudflare and Anthropic act on our instructions as processors, and both may process data in the United States.

You can turn the cloud coach off in Settings at any time. Steplight then goes back to the built-in coach, which runs entirely on your phone.

Please don't put other people's private details, or anything you would not want to leave your phone, into a task or a brain dump you use the cloud coach on.

Purchases

Steplight Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether you have Plus. We never receive your name, your email address or your card details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. No notification passes through our servers.

If you write to us

If you email support@leantensify.com we receive your email address and whatever you tell us, and we use it only to answer you. We keep support mail for up to two years.

Why we are allowed to process this

For support mail: our legitimate interest in answering you. For the cloud coach: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. For everything stored on your phone, we are not processing it at all; you are.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you, object to our use of it, and complain to a supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ, uoou.gov.cz). Since Steplight holds almost nothing about you, the fastest way to delete your data is in the app.

Children

Steplight is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change applies to you.
