Support

Write to support@leantensify.com. A person reads every message, usually within two working days.

Steplight Plus

How do I cancel? On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Steplight. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles billing. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Plus and it isn't showing. In Steplight, open Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

What stays free? The walk, the focus timer, your companion, capturing thoughts, the three starter routines, breathing, deciding, and every accessibility setting.

Your data

Where is my data? On your iPhone. Steplight has no account and no server that holds your tasks.

How do I delete everything? In Steplight: Settings › Erase everything. Deleting the app does the same.

Will I lose my lanterns if I stop for a while? No. Lanterns stay lit however long you are away.

Reminders and the timer

I don't get reminders. Check Settings › Notifications › Steplight on your iPhone, and the quiet hours inside Steplight's own settings.

The timer isn't on my Lock Screen. Allow Live Activities in Settings › Steplight.

One more thing

Steplight is a planning aid, not medical care. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
