Light the path, one small step at a time.

Steplight is an iPhone app for brains that know what to do and still can't start.

One step on screen. Add the thing you've been putting off. Steplight breaks it into small steps and shows you only the first.

A path that lights up. Every step you finish lights a lantern on a walk through quiet places. Lanterns never go out. There are no streaks to break.

Someone to start with. Your companion walks you up to the starting line in two minutes, then gets out of the way.

Time you can see. A disk that shrinks, on the screen, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. What you write stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

Not medical care

Steplight is a planning aid. It does not diagnose or treat ADHD or anything else.
